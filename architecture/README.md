## When to write here

- when the document explains platform structure rather than step-by-step execution
- when the content spans multiple repositories or teams
- when the document should remain stable even if workflows or UI details change

## When not to write here

- do not put runbooks, rollout procedures, or environment checklists here
- do not duplicate repository-local implementation detail that belongs in service repos
- do not use this section for roadmap or retrospective-only material
